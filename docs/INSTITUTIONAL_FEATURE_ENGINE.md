## 🏗️ 1. Market Structure & Regime (The "Big Picture")
*Identifies if the current environment rewards trend-following or mean-reversion strategies.*

| Feature | Calculation | Institutional Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hurst Exponent** | Rescaled Range (R/S) Analysis | **H > 0.5**: Trending (Persistent). **H < 0.5**: Mean-Reverting (Anti-persistent). **H ≈ 0.5**: Random Walk (Noise). |
| **MA Gap** | (EMA20 - EMA50) / Price | Measures the "stretch" in a trend. High values indicate overextended trends (exhaustion risk). |
| **Regime Z-Score** | Multi-Factor ADX + Slope | Standardizes how "strong" a trend is relative to its own 100-bar history. |

## ⚡ 2. Momentum & Velocity
*Measures the directional strength and "drive" behind price moves.*

| Feature | Calculation | Institutional Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ROC 20** | (Price_T / Price_T-20) - 1 | Baseline velocity over a 20-bar window. |
| **MACD Strength** | (MACD - Signal) / Norm_Vol | Normalized signal strength. Filters out "weak" crosses during low-volume periods. |
| **Autocorr (Lag-1)** | Rolling Return Correlation | Measures "Stickiness". High positive autocorr = High trend reliability. Negative = Fakeout prone. |
| **Acceleration** | 2nd Derivative of Price | Detects "Blow-off Tops" or "Climax Runs" before they reverse. |

## 📉 3. Mean Reversion & Exhaustion
*Flags when price is at an extreme and likely to snap back.*

| Feature | Calculation | Institutional Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BB Percentile** | (Price - Lower) / (Upper - Lower) | % position within Bollinger Bands. > 0.95 = Extreme Overbought. < 0.05 = Extreme Oversold. |
| **VWAP Deviation** | (Price - VWAP) / Std_Dev | Measures institutional value deviation. Price usually returns to VWAP 80% of the time. |
| **RSI Percentile** | RSI relative to Min/Max | Normalizes RSI. An RSI of 70 in a raging bull market is different from 70 in a range. |
| **OBV Correlation** | corr(Price, Vol_Accum) | **Divergence Check:** If Price goes up but OBV Correlation drops, the move is fake (Distribution). |

## 🌪️ 4. Volatility & Risk
*Determines position sizing and trade viability.*

| Feature | Calculation | Institutional Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **VoV** | SD of Historical Vol | "Volatility of Volatility". High VoV indicates unstable market regimes (Stay away). |
| **Vol Z-Score** | HV relative to Avg_HV | Detects abnormal "Vol Spikes" (News events, Black Swans). |
