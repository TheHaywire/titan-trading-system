## 🎓 Key Concepts in This Plan

- **Net Score**: A simple way we rank how many positive reasons we have to trade. Higher is better for buying, lower better for selling.
- **Positioning Extreme**: When almost everyone trades the same way, the trade becomes 'fragile' and prone to reversing.
- **Seasonality**: Historical proof that certain months usually behave a certain way for specific assets.
- **MTF Alignment**: Making sure the short-term and long-term trends agree before we commit real capital.
